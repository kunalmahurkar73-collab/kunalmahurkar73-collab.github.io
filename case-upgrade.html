Case study 01 • Program ownership

Enterprise Platform Upgrade

A major enterprise-platform upgrade where technical execution was only part of the challenge. The harder product problem was creating shared readiness across customer, engineering and R&D dependencies.

Scope5 environments
Delivery window13 weeks
Domain3DEXPERIENCE
FocusReadiness & risk

Customer/employer-sensitive details are intentionally summarized.

01 — The problem

What had to change?

Moving a mature enterprise platform across multiple environments creates interconnected risks: compatibility, testing, environment sequencing, stakeholder dependencies and recovery readiness. A technically successful install is not enough if the wider system is not ready for go-live.

02 — My role

Where I contributed.

My role was to own and coordinate the technical upgrade journey: connect the customer, development contributors and R&D dependencies; make risks visible; align milestones; and drive testing and readiness rather than treating each environment as an isolated installation.

03 — Approach

Turn ambiguity into decisions.

Make dependencies visible

Mapped the work that depended on customer, engineering and R&D so hidden blockers surfaced early.

Treat recovery as product risk

Brought disaster-recovery feasibility into the readiness conversation instead of leaving it as a late infrastructure concern.

Create one operating picture

Used shared milestones and ownership so different contributors were working toward the same definition of ready.

Validate before confidence

Coordinated testing across environments and used evidence, not assumption, to move toward go-live.

04 — Outcome

The upgrade moved across five environments in a 13-week window, turning a technically complex program into a coordinated delivery story.

05 — Product lens

What I learned.

The strongest lesson was that enterprise delivery behaves like product management at scale: the job is not simply to complete technical tasks. It is to understand the system of users, teams, risks and dependencies and continuously reduce uncertainty until everyone can make a confident release decision.
