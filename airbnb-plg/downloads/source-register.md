# Source and reference register

Independent Airbnb India PM/PLG case study by Kunal Mahurkar. Reviewed 27 November 2026.

This independent product management case study currently uses publicly available secondary research and a clearly labelled synthetic customer dataset to demonstrate research analysis, hypothesis formation, feature prioritization and MVP design. Primary customer research is ongoing. Synthetic responses are not actual participant responses and are excluded from verified research findings. Product recommendations will be reassessed as genuine customer and host feedback becomes available.

## Evidence rules

Only the claim stated for each source is supported. Availability, pricing and inventory can change. Company releases are first-party claims, not independent customer research. Undated competitor pages are timestamped observations, not publication-date estimates. Do not translate tourist visits into unique travellers, bookings or a market-share estimate.

## What is proposed

The four MVPs are proposed orchestration extensions: explicit private budgets and availability reconciliation; opt-in reusable group constraints; explainable group-readiness actions; and optional activity bundles. Wishlists, voting, messaging, social connections, home comparison, experiences and host insights are existing or announced Airbnb capabilities. Geographic rollout and account availability need manual India verification before a production proposal.

## Financial boundary

The default 40,000 bookings, 10% uplift, ?12,000 booking value, 15.5% effective fee, ?16 lakh experience revenue, costs and scenario ranges are illustrative assumptions. They are not Airbnb India internal data. The revenue calculator is a learning model rather than an audited accounting statement.
